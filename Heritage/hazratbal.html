Hazratbal Shrine

Description

Hazratbal is a revered mosque on the northern shore of Dal Lake, known for its white marble façade and spiritual significance to many visitors.

History

The shrine houses a relic believed by many to be a hair of the Prophet Muhammad. Over centuries it has been a center of devotion and community gatherings, with a calm lakeside setting.

Respectful attire is requested when visiting the shrine.
