Mughal Gardens

Description

The Mughal Gardens of Srinagar — including Shalimar Bagh, Nishat Bagh, and Chashme Shahi — are terraced gardens laid out in the Persian-influenced charbagh style. They are celebrated for their symmetry, water channels, and seasonal blossoms.

History

Commissioned by Mughal emperors in the 16th and 17th centuries, these gardens were designed as royal retreats. They reflect the Mughal love for horticulture and water features, and remain among the most visited heritage landscapes in Kashmir.

Best time to visit: spring and early autumn for blooms and mild weather.
