Dal Lake

Description

Dal Lake is the iconic water heart of Srinagar, known for its houseboats, shikaras, and floating gardens. It is a place of gentle movement and reflection.

History

For centuries Dal Lake has been central to local life and culture. Houseboats became popular in the 19th and 20th centuries, offering a unique way to stay on the water.

Shikara rides are best at sunrise or sunset for calm waters and soft light.
