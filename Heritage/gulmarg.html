Gulmarg

Description

Gulmarg is a meadowland and hill station known for its gentle slopes, wildflowers, and winter snow. It is a peaceful place for walks and quiet contemplation.

History

Once a royal hunting ground, Gulmarg developed into a resort area in the 19th and 20th centuries. Its natural beauty has long drawn visitors seeking calm and fresh air.

Seasonal note: wildflowers in summer, snow in winter.
